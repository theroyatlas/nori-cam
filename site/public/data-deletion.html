Data deletion

You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you.

On this website

We do not store your IP address, your edits or your options on the server (see privacy). The only visitor data is a salted hash counter that disappears by itself within a day, and cached answers that disappear within 24 hours. Your browser copy of your edits can be cleared by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

On a social platform

If a comment or username of yours appears on Nori’s posts or in the journal and you want it removed, contact us with a link to it and we will remove it. Deleting your own comment on the platform also removes it from the platform; Nori keeps no separate copy of comments on this website.

How to ask

Write to CONTACT_PLACEHOLDER (a contact address will be added here before launch). Say what you want deleted and where you saw it. We reply and confirm when it is done.
