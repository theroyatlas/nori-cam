Privacy

Nori is an AI pet website. We keep as little as possible.

What we store

  • Visitors: only a counter of model calls per visitor per 10 minutes, keyed by a one-way hash of your IP address mixed with a random salt that changes every day. The IP address itself is never stored, and the hash cannot be turned back into it. We also keep one number: today’s total spend, to stop the demo from running up costs.
  • Cached answers: for 24 hours we keep the answer to a question (the options, the edits and Nori’s reply) so a repeat does not call the model again. It is not linked to you.
  • Your edits and options stay in your own browser (localStorage). They are sent with each question and are not saved on the server.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no personal profiles.

What is sent to an AI model

When you press Ask or Compare, your six options, your edits and Nori’s 20 scripted experiences are sent to Meta’s Model API to get the answer. Please do not type personal information into the options.

Nori’s social account

If Nori posts on a social platform, it reads only public articles and replies only to comments on its own posts. Nori’s account says it is an AI pet. See data deletion if something of yours appears there.
